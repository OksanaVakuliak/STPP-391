About the Game

A royal match-3 expedition across the globe

Royal Journey follows King William on a grand quest, traveling through enchanting cities and iconic landmarks while solving classic match-3 puzzles. With thousands of levels and constantly expanding destinations, players uncover hidden stories tied to each location while enjoying seamless offline play.

How to Play

Simple matches, worldwide adventure

  • Swap & Match Tiles

    Match three or more identical items to clear the board.

  • Travel Between Cities

    Progress unlocks new destinations around the world.

  • Complete Level Objectives

    Solve puzzles to advance King William's journey.

  • Collect Royal Rewards

    Earn treasures and bonuses as you progress.

  • Play Anytime, Anywhere

    Enjoy offline play with no connection required.
